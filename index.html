Privacy Policy

For ConstructQ: Construction Calc
Effective September 30, 2026

ConstructQ: Construction Calc ("ConstructQ", "the app") is a construction measurement and estimating utility. This policy describes how the first, offline-only release handles information.

Information you enter

Measurements, quantities, unit preferences and calculation inputs are processed locally on your Android device. The app does not upload those inputs to a ConstructQ server. The current version has no account registration, cloud synchronization, or remote storage of your calculation inputs.

Personal information and data sharing

The current offline-only version does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information, and ConstructQ does not sell or share users' calculation inputs. If future releases introduce accounts, online services, analytics, advertising or purchase verification, this policy and the app's Google Play Data safety declaration will be reviewed and updated before release.

Advertising and purchases

The current offline-only version does not integrate advertising or in-app purchasing services. ConstructQ may add optional advertising and a paid Pro upgrade in a future version. If these services are introduced, their data handling and any applicable privacy controls will be disclosed before they are enabled.

Permissions and device services

Core calculator functions do not require internet access or access to the device's camera, microphone, location or contacts in the current version. Android and Google Play may separately process information related to installation, updates, device security and platform services under their own policies.

Data retention and deletion

Calculator inputs in the current version are used during an active app session. You may clear the app's data using your device's Android settings or uninstall the app. ConstructQ does not maintain a remote account or a server-side store of your calculator measurements.

Children

ConstructQ is designed for adults using construction and DIY calculation tools. It is not designed for children.

Security

Keeping the core calculation features offline helps minimize unnecessary transmission of input data. No device or system can be guaranteed to be entirely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated for material changes.
