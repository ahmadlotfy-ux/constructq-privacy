Privacy Policy

For ConstructQ: Construction Calc
Effective September 30, 2026

ConstructQ: Construction Calc ("ConstructQ", "the app") is a construction measurement and estimating utility. This policy describes how ConstructQ handles information and explains additional data processing when advertising is enabled in an installed version of the app.

Core calculations and information you enter

Measurements, quantities, unit preferences and calculator inputs are processed on your Android device. ConstructQ does not send these calculation inputs to a ConstructQ server. Core calculators do not require a ConstructQ account. Ad services, if enabled in your installed version, process different information as explained below.

Advertising (versions that include ads)

When advertising is enabled in your installed version, ConstructQ uses Google AdMob to show ads. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information such as your device's IP address (which can be used to estimate general location), interactions with ads and the app, performance and diagnostic information, and device or advertising identifiers where available and permitted. Google and selected advertising partners may process this information to deliver ads, measure effectiveness, analyze performance, and detect fraud and abuse. Whether ads are personalized depends on the applicable consent and privacy choices.

ConstructQ does not intentionally send the measurements you enter in its calculators to AdMob. Advertising services are operated by third parties under their own privacy policies and retention rules. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's advertising privacy information. Any additional advertising partners used by the app should be disclosed through its consent interface where applicable.

Advertising consent and your choices

Where required by law, ConstructQ will request advertising consent through a consent-management interface before requesting ads. Where required, the app will also offer a way to review or change privacy choices, typically through its settings. You can also manage or reset the advertising identifier using your Android device's privacy settings and manage Google ad personalization through My Ad Center. The availability and effect of these controls may vary by device, region, app version, and consent status.

Purchases

A paid Pro upgrade is planned but is not active in the initial project version. If enabled in a later release, purchase transactions will be handled by Google Play; ConstructQ will not receive your full payment-card details. The app may process the purchase status needed to provide and restore access to Pro features. This policy will be reviewed when the final purchase flow is implemented.

Permissions and device services

Core calculations can run offline and do not require camera, microphone, precise location or contacts access. In an ad-supported version, showing ads and requesting consent require an internet connection, and the advertising SDK may access permitted identifiers and network-related information. Android and Google Play may separately process installation, update, security and platform-service information under their own policies.

Data retention and deletion

Calculator inputs are processed locally. You may clear the app's local data in Android settings or uninstall it. ConstructQ does not maintain a remote account or server-side database of your calculator measurements. Google AdMob and its partners, where enabled, retain advertising-related information according to their own policies. Contact us at the address below if you have questions about your data.

Children

ConstructQ is designed for adults using construction and DIY calculation tools. It is not designed for children.

Security

Core calculator inputs remain local. Advertising services, where enabled, transmit data for ads and related services. No device or system can be guaranteed to be entirely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated for material changes.

Contact

If you have a privacy question about ConstructQ, email constructqsupport@gmail.com.
